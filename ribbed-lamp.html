Ribbed lamp

Lighting / Product concept

Form study

Drag or use arrow keys to rotate

Relative dimensions · H = 100

Illustrative reconstruction from the concept render.

Concept

What if the shade and the base shared a single outline? Here, vertical ribs run from the crown to the foot. Their changing profile gives the lamp a broad body and a narrow place to meet the table.

Process

Follow one rib down the shade. Near the middle it runs almost straight. Towards the foot it turns inward, bringing the ribs closer together. That change in spacing gives the eye a way to read the curve.

Outcome

In the visualization, the brightest bands sit between the ribs. The outer edges stay in shadow, so the shape remains legible even where the shade glows. A physical light test would show how closely the real object follows this study.
