Modular Laptop Arm

Desk accessories / Concept study

Lift the laptop. Free the desk.

Form study

In the position shown, the Fusion assembly spans approximately 486 mm in width, 394 mm in depth and 482 mm in height.

Concept

A clamp at the edge of the desk carries the laptop above the work surface. The articulated arm brings the screen closer while leaving space beneath the platform for a keyboard.

Process

Modelled as an assembly in Autodesk Fusion, the stand connects the desk clamp to the laptop platform through a series of pivot joints. Rotate the model to see how the links meet and how the clamp sits beneath the desktop.

Outcome

In the desk study, the laptop sits beside the monitor with room for a keyboard underneath. The clamp and pivot joints remain visible, so the way the platform is supported can be read from the side.
