Amber

Table lamp / Concept study

Concept

Amber is a reference-based table lamp study. A broad, rounded shade narrows into a cylindrical body. The colour runs almost to the table, where a short band of satin steel catches the light.

Process

The profile was modelled in Fusion and brought into Blender to study the amber finish and the reflection along the metal base.

Dimensions

Height 250 mm · Shade diameter approx. 200 mm · Base diameter 114 mm
